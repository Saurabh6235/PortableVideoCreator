Topic: Why Do Most Retail Traders Lose Money?
Title: 90% Traders Lose Money! But Why?
Hook: "Stock market mein paisa kamane aaye the... phir 90% log apna paisa kyun gawa dete hain?"
Hidden science: Trading psychology, emotional decision-making, overtrading, leverage, transaction costs, risk-reward ratio.
Interesting fact: SEBI's study of individual equity F&O traders found that 93% incurred losses during FY22–FY24.

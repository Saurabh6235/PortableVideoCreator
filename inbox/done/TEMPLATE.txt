Topic: How Can Radar See Rain Before It Reaches You?
Title: How Does Weather Radar See Invisible Rain in the Sky?
Hook: "Aasmaan mein door baarish ho rahi hai… lekin radar ko kaise pata chal jaata hai?"
Hidden science: Electromagnetic waves, reflection, Doppler shift and precipitation measurement.
Interesting fact: Doppler weather radar can estimate the movement of precipitation and help identify rotating storm patterns.
